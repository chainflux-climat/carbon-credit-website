The problem

Most validation findings are predictable.

They are found weeks into validation, by the validator, at the validator's day rate. Climat is built to find them while the draft is still on your desk.

A claim with no source

An adoption rate, a yield, a cost. Stated once in the additionality section, sourced nowhere. It reads as fact until someone asks for the document behind it.

Two sections that disagree

The boundary says one area. The quantification uses another. Each section was right when it was written, and nobody read them side by side.

A condition nobody addressed

A methodology lists its applicability conditions one by one. A project document that answers most of them has not answered the methodology.

How it works

Seven steps, in the order the work actually happens.

  1. Choose the registry and methodology

    Pick Verra VCS or Gold Standard, then the methodology. The intake form is generated from the inputs that methodology requires, so you are asked only what matters.

  2. Add your evidence

    Upload surveys, land records, financial models and studies as PDF, Word or text. A file that cannot be read is flagged. It is never quietly counted as evidence.

  3. Check applicability

    Every applicability condition is evaluated as pass, fail or needs review. Conditions that call for judgment go to a person.

  4. See what is missing

    An evidence gap report lists what the project still has to prove, blocking gaps first, before any drafting starts.

  5. Draft with citations

    Each section is drafted in the registry's own order, with citations to your intake, your evidence or the methodology.

  6. Review and finalize

    Read each section, edit it, or ask for a rewrite with your own instructions. Once you finalize a section it is never regenerated.

  7. Export for verification

    The finished case is assembled as one document, with a cover note for your validation body that states plainly that AI assistance was used.

Evidence gaps

See what the project still has to prove.

Before any drafting starts, Climat lists the evidence the case needs and does not yet have, most serious first. Drafting waits until the blocking items are supplied.

  • Blocking, required or advisory, so you know what to chase first
  • An unreadable upload is reported, not counted
  • Evidence that exists is never described as sufficient on its own
