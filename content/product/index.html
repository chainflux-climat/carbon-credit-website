Product

One workspace, from methodology choice to a finished project document.

Climat takes a carbon project through seven steps: choose the registry and methodology, complete an intake built from that methodology, upload evidence, check applicability, review the evidence gaps, draft each section with citations, then review, finalize and export. A person decides at every step.

What happens at each step?

Each step produces something you can read and check before the next one starts.

What do you have at the end?

The project document

Every section the registry requires, in the registry's order, each one finalized by a person on your team.

A trail for every claim

Citations on the statements in the draft. Any citation that could not be matched to the evidence you supplied is flagged, not hidden.

A cover note

A note for the validation and verification body that states plainly that AI assistance was used in preparing the document.

What will Climat not do?

The limits are part of the product.

It will not invent evidence

Where a document is missing, the draft says evidence is needed. It does not supply a plausible number.

It will not decide for you

Conditions that need judgment are routed to a person. Sections are final only when a person finalizes them.

It will not replace validation

Climat is not a registry and not a validation body. It does not guarantee registration, and it does not submit on your behalf.
