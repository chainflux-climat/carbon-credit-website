What does the applicability check do?

It takes the applicability conditions of the methodology you chose and tests your project against each one. Nothing is skipped: a methodology with nine conditions produces nine results.

The check runs as soon as the intake is submitted, before any drafting. If the methodology does not fit, you find out before you have spent time on a document.

What do the three results mean?

ResultMeaningWhat to do
PassThe input you gave satisfies the condition as the methodology states it.Make sure the evidence behind the input is uploaded. The validator will ask for it.
FailThe input you gave does not satisfy the condition.Check the input. If it is correct, the project does not fit this methodology as described.
Needs reviewThe condition calls for judgment, or its input has not been supplied.A person on your team has to make the determination and record why.

Which conditions are sent to a person?

Some conditions are thresholds: a density, a date, a capacity. Those can be computed. Others ask whether an arrangement meets a description, and turn on facts a form cannot capture. Climat marks those as needs review and does not attempt an answer.

For background on how validators read these conditions, see the guide to applicability conditions.

Which methodologies does it cover?

The check covers the methodologies Climat supports today: Verra VM0047 version 1.1, census-based approach, and Verra VMR0017 version 1.0. Conditions are set up from the published methodology text.
