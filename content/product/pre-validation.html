What is pre-validation?

Pre-validation is a review of the project document before it goes to the validator. Its purpose is to find the issues a validation and verification body is likely to raise, while they are still cheap to fix. A finding raised during validation costs a round trip: the validator writes it up, the developer responds, the validator reviews the response. For how that process runs, see validation and verification.

What will Climat's review do?

It will read the finished draft adversarially, one section at a time, with the methodology's own conditions and the other sections in view. Each finding will have four parts.

PartWhat it contains
ClaimThe exact words of the document that are being challenged, quoted word for word
IssueWhat is wrong or unsupported, and why a validator would raise it
RemedyWhat would resolve it: a source, a correction, a document
SeverityBlocking, material or advisory

How will findings be graded?

SeverityMeaning
BlockingThe document would fail validation on this point alone
MaterialLikely to be raised by a validator, and will cost a round trip
AdvisoryWould strengthen the case, but is not a likely finding

What kinds of problem will it look for?

  • Unsupported claim. Something asserted with no citation.
  • Evidence gap. Something cited to evidence that does not exist yet.
  • Methodology deviation. Something that conflicts with the methodology's own rules.
  • Internal inconsistency. Something that contradicts another section or the intake.
  • Registry requirement. Something the registry asks for that the section leaves out.
  • Calculation. An arithmetic or unit error.

What is available today instead?

Three checks that catch many of the same problems earlier are available now: the applicability check, the evidence gap report, and the citation checks and conflict warnings in document drafting.
