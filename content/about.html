What is Climat?

Climat is software for the people who prepare carbon projects for registration. It checks a project against the methodology, reports what evidence is missing, and drafts the registry's project document with a citation on every claim. A person reviews and finalizes every section. See how it works.

Earlier versions of Climat covered sustainability reporting and a carbon credit marketplace. Today the product is focused on origination: getting a project from an idea to a document a validator can work with.

Who builds it?

Climat is built by Chainflux, a software company founded in 2018 and based in Bengaluru, India. The company is led by Abhinav Ramesh, founder and chief executive, and Amit Maurya, chief technology officer.

How do we work?

  • Say only what the evidence supports. A draft that reads well and cannot be sourced is a liability. Where the evidence is missing, the document says so.
  • A person signs the document, so a person decides. Software proposes. People review, edit and finalize.
  • Short lists, fully checked. We support a methodology only after reading its published text in full. We would rather cover two methodologies correctly than twenty approximately.
  • Name what is not ready. Features in development are labeled as in development, on this site and in the product.

What is Climat not?

Climat is not a registry and not a validation and verification body. It does not issue credits, does not guarantee registration, and does not replace validation or verification.
