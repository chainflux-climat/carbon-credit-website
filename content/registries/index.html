Why does the registry matter to the document?

The same project, under the same kind of methodology, produces a different document at each registry. Gold Standard asks for an assessment against its safeguarding principles, a stakeholder consultation report and stated contributions to the Sustainable Development Goals. The Verified Carbon Standard does not ask for those in the same form. A document written for one registry and relabeled for the other will be missing sections.
