How are these guides written?

Every rule, number and deadline in a guide is taken from a document published by the registry or standard-setter, and linked. Where we could not find a primary source, we left the claim out. Where a rule depends on the judgment of a validator or the registry, the guide says so.

Found something wrong?

Standards change, and we would rather be corrected than be wrong. Write to us with the page and the clause, and we will fix it and date the change.
