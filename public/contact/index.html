Contact us

Climat is in early access. Tell us which methodology you are working under and where the project stands, and we will arrange a working session on a real project.

  • Emailcontact@chainflux.com
  • Phone+91 97395 79930
  • OfficeChainflux, 42, 2nd and 3rd Floor, 100 Feet Road, Koramangala 4th Block, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560034, India

What should I include?

  • The registry and the methodology you are working under, with its version
  • The country and the type of project
  • Where the project stands: idea, feasibility, drafting, or under validation
  • What is slowing you down most

What happens next?

We reply by email and arrange a working session. If your methodology is one Climat already supports, the session uses your own project. If it is not, we will tell you plainly whether and when we expect to add it.
