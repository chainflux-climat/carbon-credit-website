Guides

Practical guides for people who prepare carbon projects for registration. Each guide answers one question, cites the registry documents it rests on, names the version of each standard it describes, and carries the date it was last checked.

  • Guide Applicability conditions: how to tell whether a methodology fitsWhat applicability conditions are, how validators test them, what to do when a project fails one, and worked examples from Verra VM0047 and VMR0017. Read →
  • Guide How to register a carbon projectThe sequence for registering a carbon project under Verra VCS and Gold Standard, step by step, with the deadlines and fees each registry has published. Read →
  • Guide Validation vs verification in carbon projectsHow validation differs from verification, how Verra and Gold Standard approve validation/verification bodies, what each finding means, and what the report contains. Read →

How are these guides written?

Every rule, number and deadline in a guide is taken from a document published by the registry or standard-setter, and linked. Where we could not find a primary source, we left the claim out. Where a rule depends on the judgment of a validator or the registry, the guide says so.

Found something wrong?

Standards change, and we would rather be corrected than be wrong. Write to us with the page and the clause, and we will fix it and date the change.
