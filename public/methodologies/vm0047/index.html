VM0047 explained: applicability, additionality, baseline and monitoring

VM0047 is Verra's methodology for afforestation, reforestation and revegetation. Version 1.1, active since 14 May 2025, credits carbon dioxide removals under two mutually exclusive approaches: an area-based approach that uses a remote-sensing performance benchmark, and a census-based approach for dispersed planting of up to 50 planting units per hectare with a zero baseline.

What does VM0047 cover, and which version is current?

VM0047 quantifies carbon dioxide removals from afforestation, reforestation and revegetation (ARR) under Verra's Verified Carbon Standard. The current version is VM0047 v1.1, issued on 14 May 2025. Verra's methodology page listed it as active, under sectoral scope 14, when checked on 29 September 2026.

Version 1.0 is closed to new projects. The same page sets a grace period: projects using v1.0 had to request pipeline listing as under validation by 31 December 2025 and must request registration by 31 December 2026.

What is the difference between the area-based and census-based approaches?

The area-based approach measures carbon per hectare and credits only growth beyond a benchmark observed on control plots. The census-based approach counts individual planting units against a zero baseline. The two are mutually exclusive for a project activity instance, and the choice made at the project start date holds for the whole crediting period (Section 4.1).

Which project activities are eligible, and which are excluded?

Eligible activities establish, increase or restore vegetative cover by direct planting, seeding or assisted natural regeneration (Section 4). Section 4.4 lists exclusions and describes the list as non-exhaustive:

  • Area-based: land that met the definition of managed forest at any point in the 10 years before the project start date; clearing of pre-existing woody biomass that involves timber harvesting or degrades native ecosystems; planting below 50 units per hectare that could use the census-based approach.
  • Census-based: sites where woody biomass serving a similar purpose to the planting units was removed in the last 10 years; soil inversion deeper than 25 cm.

Every project meets the five general conditions, then all conditions of the approach it applies. The table paraphrases Section 4 of version 1.1.

Section 4.1(4) dates the project start from site preparation or land use change, while Section 4.3(4) says the census marks the start. Settle both readings with the validation body.

How is additionality demonstrated under VM0047?

Area-based projects use a performance method and census-based projects a project method (Section 7). Both show regulatory surplus under the VCS Program rules.

  • Performance benchmark (area-based). The stocking index trend on project plots must differ significantly from the trend on matched control plots: a Z value of 1.96 or more in absolute terms. It is shown ex ante at validation and retested at each verification.
  • Investment analysis. Benchmark or investment comparison analysis under Step 3 of VT0008. Census-based projects always need it. Area-based projects skip it only where they have no revenue or incentive other than carbon credit sales.
  • Common practice (census-based). Similar landowners are surveyed within five years of the start date. Adoption below 15% among landowners without carbon finance means the activity is not common practice. At 15% or above the project is not additional (Section 7.3.4).

How is the baseline set?

The area-based baseline is the performance benchmark: the ratio of the stocking index trend on control plots to the trend on project plots (Appendix 1). At least 30 project plots of 0.01 to 10 hectares are sampled. Control plots share the jurisdiction, ecoregion, policy environment and land tenure class, lie within 100 km at first, and are matched on ten years of stocking index history. Where the trends do not differ significantly, the benchmark is set to 1.

The census-based baseline is zero where the site has under 10% pre-existing woody biomass cover and is continuously cropped or classed as settlements or other lands (Section 6.2). Continuous cropping means cultivation of the same site every year for 10 years or more, with no fallow longer than one season.

How are removals quantified?

Both approaches use stock difference: stock at this monitoring event minus stock at the previous one, net of uncertainty and project emissions (Section 8.6). Area-based projects also deduct the benchmark and leakage.

What must be monitored, and how often?

Field parameters are monitored every five years or more frequently, and the stocking index at least annually (Section 9.2 and Appendix 1).

Which tools and modules does VM0047 call on?

  • VMD0054, the leakage module, in its most recent version. Verra lists v1.1 as active since 13 January 2026.
  • VT0008 Additionality Assessment, Step 3.
  • The VCS Standard, for the long-term average where the project harvests and for the non-permanence risk report (Section 3.2.11). See the Verra VCS page.

What is commonly challenged at validation?

This list is not based on validation statistics. It collects the points where the methodology demands evidence or leaves room for judgment.

  • Start date and t = 0. Dated evidence, and plot measurements inside the two-year windows.
  • Land history. Photos or attestations that no similar woody biomass was removed in 10 years.
  • Common practice. Whether the sample is representative and the geographic domain is drawn correctly.
  • Allometric equations. Global equations must rest on destructive sampling from the same ecoregion or Holdridge life zone.
  • Census records. Unique ID, geo-referenced point, planting year and species for every unit.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the VM0047 census-based approach for assisted natural regeneration?

No. The census-based approach covers direct planting only (Section 4.3). Assisted natural regeneration is eligible under the area-based approach, which requires a performance benchmark built from matched control plots and a leakage calculation under VMD0054.

What is the maximum planting density under the VM0047 census-based approach?

Fifty planting units per hectare, scaled to the size of each instance, so a half-hectare instance may hold 25 units. In larger instances planting has to be dispersed, not concentrated in one part. Existing vegetation does not count toward the limit.

Does a census-based VM0047 project have to quantify leakage?

No. Leakage is set to zero under the census-based approach, because the pre-project land use has to continue and planting density is capped (Section 8.4). Area-based projects must quantify leakage with module VMD0054 and may not assume it is de minimis.

How often does VM0047 require biomass to be measured?

Field parameters such as biomass and mortality are monitored every five years or more frequently. Under the area-based approach the stocking index of project and control plots is recorded at least annually. The performance benchmark is updated at each verification or every five years, whichever comes first.

Can a project still use VM0047 version 1.0?

Only a project that was already in the pipeline. Verra's grace period required projects using version 1.0 to request pipeline listing as under validation by 31 December 2025, and they must request registration by 31 December 2026. All other projects apply version 1.1.

Which carbon pools does VM0047 count?

The census-based approach counts aboveground and belowground woody biomass only and excludes every other pool as conservative. The area-based approach always counts woody biomass. It adds non-woody biomass, dead wood, litter and soil organic carbon where the project significantly reduces them, tested with Appendix 2 of the methodology.
