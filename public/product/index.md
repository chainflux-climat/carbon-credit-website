# How Climat works: from methodology choice to export

Updated 2026-09-29. Canonical: https://climat.today/product/

Product

One workspace, from methodology choice to a finished project document.

Climat takes a carbon project through seven steps: choose the registry and methodology, complete an intake built from that methodology, upload evidence, check applicability, review the evidence gaps, draft each section with citations, then review, finalize and export. A person decides at every step.

## What happens at each step?

Each step produces something you can read and check before the next one starts.

| Step | You provide | Climat does | You get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Registry and methodology | Your choice of registry, sector and methodology | Loads the document structure of the registry and the requirements of the methodology | An intake form with only the inputs this methodology needs |
| 2. Intake and evidence | Answers to the intake, and evidence as PDF, Word or text | Extracts the text of each file and flags any file it cannot read | A record of what was supplied and what could not be used |
| 3. Applicability | Nothing further | Evaluates every applicability condition against your inputs | Pass, fail or needs review for each condition |
| 4. Evidence gaps | Nothing further | Compares what the case needs with what you supplied | A list of gaps: blocking, required and advisory |
| 5. Drafting | A decision to start | Drafts each section in the registry's order, with citations and warnings | A complete first draft you can trace |
| 6. Review | Your edits and instructions | Rewrites a section on request and keeps finalized sections untouched | Sections finalized by a person |
| 7. Export | A decision to finalize | Assembles the case and writes the cover note | One document, with AI assistance disclosed |

## What do you have at the end?

### The project document

Every section the registry requires, in the registry's order, each one finalized by a person on your team.

### A trail for every claim

Citations on the statements in the draft. Any citation that could not be matched to the evidence you supplied is flagged, not hidden.

### A cover note

A note for the validation and verification body that states plainly that AI assistance was used in preparing the document.

## What will Climat not do?

The limits are part of the product.

### It will not invent evidence

Where a document is missing, the draft says evidence is needed. It does not supply a plausible number.

### It will not decide for you

Conditions that need judgment are routed to a person. Sections are final only when a person finalizes them.

### It will not replace validation

Climat is not a registry and not a validation body. It does not guarantee registration, and it does not submit on your behalf.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I edit what Climat drafts?

Yes. Every section can be edited by hand, or rewritten by giving Climat your own instructions. Nothing is final until a person finalizes it, and a finalized section is never regenerated.

### What happens when the evidence for a claim is missing?

The evidence gap report lists it before drafting starts. If drafting goes ahead without it, the section says that evidence is needed instead of stating a figure that nothing supports.

### Can Climat draft for a methodology that is not listed?

No. Climat drafts only for methodologies that have been set up from the published methodology text. Today that is Verra VM0047 version 1.1, census-based approach, and Verra VMR0017 version 1.0. Tell us which methodology you need.

### Does Climat submit the project to the registry?

No. Climat prepares the document. Submission to the validation and verification body and to the registry stays with the project proponent.
