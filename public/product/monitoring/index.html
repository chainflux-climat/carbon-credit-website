Monitoring periods

After a project is registered, it has to collect the data its methodology requires and report it for each monitoring period. Climat's monitoring tracking is in development: it is built and tested in the engine, and is not yet available to users.

In development

What is a monitoring period?

A monitoring period is the span of time covered by one monitoring report. During it, the project collects the data its methodology lists as monitored parameters. At the end, that data is reported and verified before credits are issued for the period.

What will Climat's monitoring do?

  • Open the periods. Once a project is registered, the crediting period will be divided into monitoring periods at the cadence the project sets.
  • Carry the parameters in. Each period will hold its own copy of the methodology's monitoring parameters, with the frequency the methodology states.
  • Record values and evidence. Your team will record a value and its supporting evidence for each parameter, or a reason why a parameter does not apply.
  • Refuse an incomplete period. A period with a parameter that has no value will not be submitted, and the report will name what is outstanding.

What is available today?

Everything up to a finished project document is available now. See how Climat works.

Frequently asked questions

Is monitoring available in Climat today?

Not yet. Monitoring tracking is in development. It is built and tested in the engine, and its screens are being added.

Will Climat collect monitoring data from sensors or satellites?

No. It will track the values and evidence your team records for each parameter. It is not a measurement system.
