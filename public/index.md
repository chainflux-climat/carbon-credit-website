The problem

## Most validation findings are predictable.

They are found weeks into validation, by the validator, at the validator's day rate. Climat is built to find them while the draft is still on your desk.

### A claim with no source

An adoption rate, a yield, a cost. Stated once in the additionality section, sourced nowhere. It reads as fact until someone asks for the document behind it.

### Two sections that disagree

The boundary says one area. The quantification uses another. Each section was right when it was written, and nobody read them side by side.

### A condition nobody addressed

A methodology lists its applicability conditions one by one. A project document that answers most of them has not answered the methodology.

How it works

## Seven steps, in the order the work actually happens.

1. Choose the registry and methodology Pick Verra VCS or Gold Standard, then the methodology. The intake form is generated from the inputs that methodology requires, so you are asked only what matters.

2. Add your evidence Upload surveys, land records, financial models and studies as PDF, Word or text. A file that cannot be read is flagged. It is never quietly counted as evidence.

3. Check applicability Every applicability condition is evaluated as pass, fail or needs review. Conditions that call for judgment go to a person.

4. See what is missing An evidence gap report lists what the project still has to prove, blocking gaps first, before any drafting starts.

5. Draft with citations Each section is drafted in the registry's own order, with citations to your intake, your evidence or the methodology.

6. Review and finalize Read each section, edit it, or ask for a rewrite with your own instructions. Once you finalize a section it is never regenerated.

7. Export for verification The finished case is assembled as one document, with a cover note for your validation body that states plainly that AI assistance was used.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Climat?

Climat is an origination workspace for carbon project developers. It checks a project against the applicability conditions of the chosen methodology, reports what evidence is still missing, and drafts each section of the registry's project document from the developer's own intake and evidence, with citations. A person reviews and finalizes every section.

### Who is Climat for?

Climat is built for the people who prepare carbon projects for registration: project developers, and the consultants and in-house teams who write project documents for them. It is most useful to teams that already know their project and need the documentation to stand up to validation.

### Which registries and methodologies does Climat support?

Climat drafts to the document structure of the Verra Verified Carbon Standard project description and the Gold Standard for the Global Goals project design document. The methodologies available today are Verra VM0047 version 1.1, using the census-based approach, and Verra VMR0017 version 1.0. Gold Standard methodologies are being added.

### Does Climat replace a validation and verification body?

No. Climat is not a registry and not a validation body, and it does not guarantee registration. Its job is to get the document into a state where validation goes faster: every applicability condition addressed, every claim sourced, and contradictions resolved before the validator reads it.

### How does Climat keep AI from inventing facts?

Statements in a draft carry citations to the intake data, to excerpts of the uploaded evidence, or to the methodology. A citation to an uploaded document is checked against the excerpts actually supplied, and flagged if it does not match. Where evidence is missing, the draft says so. A person reviews and finalizes every section.
