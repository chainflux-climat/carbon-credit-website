Who we are

Contributing to the development of a sustainable way of life

We are sustainability focused blockchain products firm founded in 2018. Based out of Bengaluru and Singapore, we are a 25+ member Blockchain products company with deep expertise in public and private blockchain platforms.

Our platform- Climat, enables companies to monitor sustainability metrics, raise green finance, and list/transfer green assets. We are driven by our vision, the purpose- to enhance the standards of businesses using our sustainability solutions, by means of responsibly leveraging blockchain to create a better world.

Our Vision

The mission of our ad-hoc sustainability product, Climat.today, is to provide a reliable data and transaction layer for sustainability and sustainability-based financial products in order to accelerate the transition to net-zero throughout the whole business ecosystem.

Our Core Principles
