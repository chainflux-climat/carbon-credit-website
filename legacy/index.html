About Climat
  • Our thesis is that if the world has to restrict warming below 2C, organizations will urgently need platforms to disclose their footprint, finance transition to sustainable operations, and offset current carbon impact.
  • Blockchain is ideally suited to solve for some of the above problems through providing: a. Transparency and traceability into sustainability data; b. Enabling ”Tokenization” of sustainability based assets to increase their liquidity and distribution; c. Providing financing options such as decentralized finance, NFT based finance.
  • We have built Climat - the world’s first Climate focused Blockchain platform that will host an ecosystem of Climate specific Decentralized apps (Dapps) to bring transparency to climate based data and provide liquidity for climate specific assets.
